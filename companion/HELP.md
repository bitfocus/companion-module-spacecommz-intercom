## Spacecommz.io

https://spacecommz.io/
Production comms that are out of this world

To connect a Web interface of Spacecommz.io to Companion under Settings click on more Settings and enable the API by clicking "API disabled"

## v2.0

- New: Per-PL volume control (set / up / down) with `pl_<n>_volume` variables
- New: Volume presets use Companion's layered buttons - the label, value and gauge are positioned separately
- New: Volume presets support rotary encoders (turn to adjust, press to step up)
- Changed: Requires Companion 5.0 or later (module API 2.x)
